<p>I am a <strong>master's student at Shandong University</strong>, working on learning-based control for humanoid robots. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Inner Mongolia Agricultural University.</p>
<p>My research interests include <strong>perceptive locomotion, whole-body skill learning, and sim-to-real transfer</strong>. I aim to enable stable, adaptive robot motion in complex environments. My current work explores policy training, MuJoCo simulation, and real-robot deployment for discrete foothold terrains such as stepping piles.</p>
<div class="academic-question"><span>Research question</span><p>How can perception guide humanoid locomotion over challenging terrain, and how can learned skills transfer reliably to the real world?</p></div>
