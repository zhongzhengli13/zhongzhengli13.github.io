    <h3>Perception-Integrated Bipedal Locomotion</h3>
    <p>Exploring how environment perception can be integrated into locomotion policies for robust and adaptive bipedal walking in complex scenes.</p>
    <div class="project-tags"><span>Perception</span><span>Locomotion</span><span>Reinforcement Learning</span></div>
    <dl class="project-details">
      <div><dt>Goal</dt><dd>Enable a bipedal robot to use environmental perception for stable, adaptive walking over complex terrain.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Approach</dt><dd>Train locomotion policies with reinforcement learning, explore perception–control integration, and iterate through deployment and debugging on the real robot.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Progress</dt><dd>Policy training, real-robot deployment, and debugging have been carried out. The videos below show interim locomotion demonstrations.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>My contribution</dt><dd>I carried out the full workflow from policy training to real-robot deployment and debugging. I received assistance during the initial real-robot debugging stage and independently handled subsequent debugging and iterations.</dd></div>
    </dl>
